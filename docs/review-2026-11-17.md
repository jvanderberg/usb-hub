## Open: minor, not fixed

- **LM5148 VIN pin 12 has no local decoupling capacitor.** Datasheet s9.4.1.3, p55, asks for one close to the pin.
  The nearest is C30 (10 uF), 6.8 mm of track away plus a via. VIN only feeds the internal VCC regulator, about 25 mA
  of gate-drive average; the 3.3 A gate pulses come from C44, the VCC cap, which sits at the pin. There is no room for
  an 0402 between pin 12 and the Q30 gate trace without reworking the controller placement.
- **The TPS54202 input cap C14 returns through L2.** Its GND reaches pin 1 through 2 vias and 4.9 mm of L2,
  because the SW pin sits between VIN and GND on SOT-23-6. Already recorded as a deviation (layout-compliance
  row 33).
- **No SW-node RC snubber footprint.** Datasheet p55 says to add one if the ringing at SW measures too high on the
  bench.
- **USB pairs have short uncoupled segments.** Each receptacle's D+/D- row tie (about 1 mm on L4, about 7 ps against
  a 500 ps edge) and each crossover hop. The hops have GND vias 0.6 mm away. Each pair also runs locally coplanar
  near the 0.5 mm-pitch hub pins. JLC's calculator gives about 88 ohm for the coupled run. Acceptable for USB 2.0 HS.
- **Seven hub A crossovers could go.** Strapping the USB2517 PRT_SWP pins would remove 7 crossover hops; this is a
  schematic change.
- **Paste windows clear the via drills by 0.05-0.07 mm.** That is paste_clear.py's default margin.
- **Test points and status LEDs have no silk text.** Their references are on F.Fab only (refs are set to fab).

## Checked and fine (reviewer and pack)

- Straps checked against the datasheets: TPS25730 ADCIN 1-4, USB2517 / USB2514B CFG / NON_REM / PRTPWR pull-downs,
  reset supervisor, VBUS_DET dividers. Also checked: PRTPWR polarity against the SY6863 EN level, OCS against OCB
  (open drain with the hub's internal pull-up), CC Rp 10 k (3 A) on the ports and Rd 5.1 k on HOST, and the
  connector pinouts.
- PD CC lines: 300 kbps, about 15 mm, TVS 1.6 mm from the connector. Total CC capacitance is 353-397 pF, inside the
  200-480 pF window the TPS25730 allows.
- Power: 5V_BUS worst drop 31 mV at 17 A, max via 0.80 A; VIN 10 mV; 3V3 31 mV.
- Edge stitching: gaps over 10 mm only at the four M3 corners (13.1 mm).
- Pair skew at the hub/port ends is under 0.02 mm. The pack's 4.22 mm skew is an artefact: it counts the
  receptacle's second-row stub.

## Problems with the analysis pack (reported back to the kicad-skills tooling)

- **check.sh** reported 0 DRC errors, while a first DRC of the same board shows 6 hole_clearance errors. Its DRC runs
  twice with --save-board, and KiCad drops the errors on the second pass.
- **netlist.md** lists R63 as not fitted. R63 is fitted in both the schematic and the board.
- **thermal.py** adds psi_JB on top of heat it already places on the exposed pad, which puts the junction about 18 K
  high for U4.
- **The hot-loop check** used the LM5148 controller's VIN pin rather than the FET power loop, because the config
  named the IC.
